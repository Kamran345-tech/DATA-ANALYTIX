# Phase 2.3 (Part A1) — Complete 14-Pack KPI Formula, Favorable Direction & Assumptions Audit (129 KPIs)

## 1. KPIs Where Lower Is Better (`favorableDirection: "down"` — 48 of 129 KPIs)

All lower-is-better KPIs (churn, CAC, CPL, CPC, DSO, NPL, defect/scrap rate, readmissions, bounce rate, latency, downtime, fraud loss, false positives, dropout rate, etc.) are configured with `favorableDirection: "down"` so negative deltas color green (`var(--success)`) and positive deltas color red (`var(--danger)`):

- **Financial & FP&A** (`financial`) -> **Debt-to-Equity** (`fin_debt_to_equity`, unit: `ratio`, favorableDirection: `down`): Debt-to-Equity = Total Debt / Total Shareholders’ Equity
- **Financial & FP&A** (`financial`) -> **Days Sales Outstanding (DSO)** (`fin_dso_days`, unit: `days`, favorableDirection: `down`): DSO = (Accounts Receivable / Total Credit Revenue) × 365 days
- **Sales & Pipeline CRM** (`sales`) -> **Avg Sales Cycle (Days)** (`avg_sales_cycle`, unit: `days`, favorableDirection: `down`): Avg Sales Cycle = Average(Close Date − Created Date in Days)
- **Sales & Pipeline CRM** (`sales`) -> **Avg Discount Rate %** (`avg_discount_rate`, unit: `percent`, favorableDirection: `down`): Avg Discount Rate (%) = Average((List Price − Net Price) / List Price × 100)
- **Marketing & Growth Attribution** (`marketing`) -> **Total Ad Spend** (`total_ad_spend`, unit: `currency`, favorableDirection: `down`): Total Ad Spend = Σ(Campaign Spend)
- **Marketing & Growth Attribution** (`marketing`) -> **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)** (`cac`, unit: `currency`, favorableDirection: `down`): CAC = Total Marketing Spend / New Customers Acquired (Conversions)
- **Marketing & Growth Attribution** (`marketing`) -> **Cost per Lead (CPL)** (`cpl`, unit: `currency`, favorableDirection: `down`): CPL = Total Ad Spend / Total Leads (MQLs)
- **Marketing & Growth Attribution** (`marketing`) -> **Cost per Click (CPC)** (`cpc`, unit: `currency`, favorableDirection: `down`): CPC = Total Ad Spend / Total Clicks
- **Customer Success & Retention** (`customer`) -> **Logo Churn Rate %** (`logo_churn_rate`, unit: `percent`, favorableDirection: `down`): Logo Churn (%) = (Churned Customers / Starting Active Customers) × 100
- **Product & SaaS Usage** (`product`) -> **Avg Response Latency (ms)** (`avg_api_latency`, unit: `count`, favorableDirection: `down`): Avg Latency (ms) = Average(Response Time in ms)
- **Product & SaaS Usage** (`product`) -> **Session Crash / Error Rate %** (`crash_rate`, unit: `percent`, favorableDirection: `down`): Crash Rate (%) = Average(Crashed Sessions / Total Sessions × 100)
- **Web & Traffic Analytics** (`web`) -> **Bounce Rate** (`bounce_rate`, unit: `percent`, favorableDirection: `down`): Bounce Rate (%) = Average(Single-Page Sessions / Total Sessions × 100)
- **Web & Traffic Analytics** (`web`) -> **Core Web Vitals LCP (ms)** (`avg_lcp_ms`, unit: `count`, favorableDirection: `down`): LCP (ms) = Average(Largest Contentful Paint in ms)
- **Operations & Supply Chain** (`operations`) -> **Avg Lead Time (Days)** (`avg_lead_time`, unit: `days`, favorableDirection: `down`): Avg Lead Time = Average(Order-to-Delivery Calendar Days)
- **Operations & Supply Chain** (`operations`) -> **Backorder Rate** (`backorder_rate`, unit: `percent`, favorableDirection: `down`): Backorder Rate (%) = (Backordered Orders / Total Shipments) × 100
- **Operations & Supply Chain** (`operations`) -> **Cost per Shipment** (`cost_per_shipment`, unit: `currency`, favorableDirection: `down`): Cost per Shipment = Total Logistics Cost / Total Shipments
- **Operations & Supply Chain** (`operations`) -> **Total Logistics Spend** (`total_logistics_cost`, unit: `currency`, favorableDirection: `down`): Total Logistics Spend = Σ(Logistics & Freight Cost)
- **HR & People Analytics** (`hr`) -> **Attrition Rate** (`attrition_rate`, unit: `percent`, favorableDirection: `down`): Attrition Rate (%) = (Total Leavers / Average Headcount) × 100
- **HR & People Analytics** (`hr`) -> **Total Exits / Leavers** (`total_leavers`, unit: `count`, favorableDirection: `down`): Total Exits = Σ(Voluntary + Involuntary Departures)
- **HR & People Analytics** (`hr`) -> **Avg Time to Hire (Days)** (`time_to_hire`, unit: `days`, favorableDirection: `down`): Time to Hire (Days) = Average(Offer Accepted Date − Requisition Open Date)
- **HR & People Analytics** (`hr`) -> **Avg Cost per Employee** (`cost_per_employee`, unit: `currency`, favorableDirection: `down`): Avg Cost per Employee = Total Payroll Cost / Total Headcount
- **Risk, Fraud & Compliance** (`risk-fraud`) -> **Confirmed Fraud Rate (%)** (`fraud_rate_pct`, unit: `percent`, favorableDirection: `down`): Confirmed Fraud Rate (%) = (Confirmed Fraud Transactions / Total Transactions) × 100
- **Risk, Fraud & Compliance** (`risk-fraud`) -> **False Positive Rate** (`false_positive_rate`, unit: `percent`, favorableDirection: `down`): False Positive Rate (%) = (False Positives / Total Flagged Alerts) × 100
- **Risk, Fraud & Compliance** (`risk-fraud`) -> **Gross Fraud Loss** (`gross_fraud_loss`, unit: `currency`, favorableDirection: `down`): Gross Fraud Loss = Σ(Confirmed Fraud & Chargeback Loss)
- **Risk, Fraud & Compliance** (`risk-fraud`) -> **Fraud Loss / Volume (%)** (`loss_to_volume_pct`, unit: `percent`, favorableDirection: `down`): Fraud Loss Rate (%) = (Gross Fraud Loss / Total Processed Volume) × 100
- **Risk, Fraud & Compliance** (`risk-fraud`) -> **Alert Trigger Rate** (`alert_trigger_rate`, unit: `percent`, favorableDirection: `down`): Alert Trigger Rate (%) = (Flagged Transactions / Total Transactions) × 100
- **Risk, Fraud & Compliance** (`risk-fraud`) -> **Total Risk Alerts** (`total_flagged_alerts`, unit: `count`, favorableDirection: `down`): Total Risk Alerts = Σ(Flagged Transactions)
- **Risk, Fraud & Compliance** (`risk-fraud`) -> **Avg ML Risk Score** (`avg_risk_score`, unit: `score`, favorableDirection: `down`): Avg ML Risk Score = Average(Transaction Risk Score)
- **Healthcare & Clinical Ops** (`healthcare`) -> **30-Day Readmission Rate** (`readmission_rate`, unit: `percent`, favorableDirection: `down`): 30-Day Readmission Rate (%) = (30-Day Readmissions / Total Admissions) × 100
- **Healthcare & Clinical Ops** (`healthcare`) -> **Avg Length of Stay (ALOS)** (`avg_alos`, unit: `days`, favorableDirection: `down`): ALOS (Days) = Average(Inpatient Days per Admission)
- **Healthcare & Clinical Ops** (`healthcare`) -> **Avg ER Wait Time (Mins)** (`er_wait_time`, unit: `days`, favorableDirection: `down`): Avg ER Wait Time (Mins) = Average(Provider Evaluation Time − Triage Arrival Time)
- **Healthcare & Clinical Ops** (`healthcare`) -> **Cost per Encounter** (`cost_per_admission`, unit: `currency`, favorableDirection: `down`): Cost per Encounter = Total Clinical Treatment Cost / Total Admissions
- **Healthcare & Clinical Ops** (`healthcare`) -> **Total Clinical Cost** (`total_clinical_cost`, unit: `currency`, favorableDirection: `down`): Total Clinical Cost = Σ(Treatment & Care Delivery Costs)
- **Healthcare & Clinical Ops** (`healthcare`) -> **30-Day Readmissions** (`total_readmissions`, unit: `count`, favorableDirection: `down`): 30-Day Readmissions = Σ(Unplanned 30-Day Readmissions)
- **Retail & E-Commerce** (`retail`) -> **Return Rate (%)** (`return_rate_pct`, unit: `percent`, favorableDirection: `down`): Return Rate (%) = (Returned Value / Total GMV) × 100
- **Retail & E-Commerce** (`retail`) -> **Markdown Rate (%)** (`markdown_rate`, unit: `percent`, favorableDirection: `down`): Markdown Rate (%) = (Total Markdowns / Total GMV) × 100
- **Manufacturing & Plant Quality** (`manufacturing`) -> **Scrap & Defect Rate (%)** (`scrap_rate`, unit: `percent`, favorableDirection: `down`): Scrap Rate (%) = (Scrap & Defect Units / Total Produced Units) × 100
- **Manufacturing & Plant Quality** (`manufacturing`) -> **Unplanned Downtime (Hrs)** (`total_downtime`, unit: `count`, favorableDirection: `down`): Unplanned Downtime (Hrs) = Σ(Equipment Breakdown Hours)
- **Manufacturing & Plant Quality** (`manufacturing`) -> **Cost per Good Unit** (`unit_production_cost`, unit: `currency`, favorableDirection: `down`): Cost per Good Unit = Total Manufacturing Cost / Good Conforming Units
- **Manufacturing & Plant Quality** (`manufacturing`) -> **Avg Cycle Time (Sec)** (`avg_cycle_time`, unit: `days`, favorableDirection: `down`): Avg Cycle Time (Sec) = Average(Unit Processing Time in Seconds)
- **Manufacturing & Plant Quality** (`manufacturing`) -> **Scrap & Rejected Units** (`total_scrap_units`, unit: `count`, favorableDirection: `down`): Scrap Units = Σ(Rejected & Defective Units)
- **Manufacturing & Plant Quality** (`manufacturing`) -> **Total Manufacturing Cost** (`total_plant_cost`, unit: `currency`, favorableDirection: `down`): Total Manufacturing Cost = Σ(Direct Plant Production Cost)
- **Banking, Lending & Insurance** (`banking-insurance`) -> **Loan-to-Deposit Ratio (LDR)** (`loan_to_deposit_ratio`, unit: `percent`, favorableDirection: `down`): LDR (%) = (Gross Loan Portfolio / Total Customer Deposits) × 100
- **Banking, Lending & Insurance** (`banking-insurance`) -> **Non-Performing Loan (NPL %)** (`npl_ratio`, unit: `percent`, favorableDirection: `down`): NPL Ratio (%) = (Non-Performing Loan Balance / Gross Loan Portfolio) × 100
- **Banking, Lending & Insurance** (`banking-insurance`) -> **Insurance Claims Loss Ratio** (`claims_loss_ratio`, unit: `percent`, favorableDirection: `down`): Claims Loss Ratio (%) = (Incurred Claims Paid / Earned Premiums) × 100
- **Banking, Lending & Insurance** (`banking-insurance`) -> **Delinquent / NPL Balance** (`npl_exposure_usd`, unit: `currency`, favorableDirection: `down`): NPL Exposure = Σ(Non-Performing Loan Balances)
- **Education & EdTech Analytics** (`education`) -> **Student Dropout Rate** (`dropout_rate`, unit: `percent`, favorableDirection: `down`): Dropout Rate (%) = (Withdrawn Students / Total Enrolled Students) × 100
- **Education & EdTech Analytics** (`education`) -> **Instruction Cost / Student** (`cost_per_student`, unit: `currency`, favorableDirection: `down`): Instruction Cost per Student = Total Instruction Cost / Total Enrolled Students
